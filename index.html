About Pooches-N-Pals

Since 2005, Pooches-N-Pals has been Henderson's trusted grooming destination. Owned by Charlie and Jin, we specialize in boutique expert grooming with a focus on your pet's comfort and well-being.

Our master groomer Jin brings exceptional attention to detail and professional standards to every appointment. We treat every dog like family and ensure they leave looking and feeling their best.
